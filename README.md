# Legal Procedure Assistant – RAG System

## Overview

This project explores the development of an intelligent assistant designed to support the interpretation of complex procedural and regulatory documents used within companies.

Organizations often rely on large internal documents that define rules, processes, and constraints. Manually navigating these documents can be time-consuming and prone to interpretation errors.

The objective of this project is to investigate how **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** can assist professionals by retrieving relevant information from such documents and generating contextual explanations or answers.

## Objectives

* Facilitate access to information contained in long regulatory documents
* Assist experts in interpreting procedures and rules
* Reduce time spent searching for relevant articles or sections
* Explore the potential of AI systems in regulatory decision support

## Data Sources

The system works with internal procedural documents containing articles, rules, and operational guidelines. These documents serve as the knowledge base used for retrieval and analysis.

## Approach

The project investigates the use of a **Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline**, combining:

* Document processing and structuring
* Information retrieval mechanisms
* Large Language Models for contextual reasoning and response generation

The goal is to experiment with different strategies for organizing and retrieving information efficiently.

## Expected Capabilities

The assistant aims to:

* Answer questions about procedures or rules
* Retrieve relevant sections of documents
* Provide explanations based on regulatory content
* Assist experts when analyzing specific cases

## Project Scope

This work is part of an academic internship project and focuses on exploring practical and research aspects of AI-assisted document analysis.

Future work may include improvements in retrieval strategies, reasoning mechanisms, and evaluation methods.
